DESCRIPTION
================================================================
SecuroBox is an on-device private media vault. Keep your videos, photos, audio and documents locked behind a PIN and Face ID. Vault files stay on your device by default. You can optionally export a passphrase-encrypted backup to a location you choose; SecuroBox never uploads files automatically.

★ Why SecuroBox

• Offline vault storage by default, with no accounts, tracking, or automatic uploads
• PIN + Face ID / Touch ID lock for the entire app
• AES-256 encryption for every file at rest
• Protected by iOS Data Protection (hardware-backed)
• PIN hashed with PBKDF2 and stored in the iOS Keychain
• No ads, no trackers, no analytics
• No account, no sign-up

★ What you can store

• Videos (MP4, MOV, M4V and more)
• Photos (JPG, PNG, HEIC, GIF, WEBP)
• Audio (MP3, M4A, WAV, AAC, FLAC)
• Documents (PDF, DOC, DOCX, PPT, XLS, TXT)

★ Built-in players

• Video player with playback speed, fullscreen and gesture controls
• Audio player with background playback
• PDF reader with smooth scrolling and zoom
• Image viewer with pinch-to-zoom

★ Organized library

• Imported media is filed by type: Videos, Photos, Audio and Documents
• Extra built-in categories you can sort into: Educational, Personal, Work, Sports, Travel
• Create your own custom folders
• Quick search across your vault
• Recycle bin for accidentally deleted files
• Sort by newest imported, oldest imported, or name
• Lock selected categories with your PIN or biometrics

★ Import options

• Capture photos and videos straight into the vault with the in-app Secure Camera
• Import existing photos, videos and audio from your library
• Move-to-vault: imported photos and videos are removed from your library and kept safely in the vault. Move them back out anytime.
• Foreground album rules with a review queue and an option to keep library originals
• Files are encrypted into a private app container, isolated from the rest of your phone

★ Security model

• PIN with strength check (rejects 1234, 0000, sequential digits, repeats)
• Optional Face ID / Touch ID unlock
• Optional in-app Face Unlock
• Auto-lock when the app goes to the background
• Privacy screen hides your vault in the App Switcher

★ Advanced protection

• Break-in detection: silently captures a photo after a wrong PIN. The photo stays inside your vault.
• Decoy PIN: opens a separate decoy vault while your real vault stays hidden.
• Offline Integrity Lock: optionally seals the vault whenever the device connects to a network.
• Optional PIN recovery using a one-time code you set up
• Guest View lets you show only selected photos or videos and requires authentication to exit

★ Backup & recovery

• Create a portable passphrase-encrypted backup and restore it on a new device
• Save backups to Files or a connected cloud drive using the system file picker
• Current backup limit: 128 MB of media

★ Privacy promise

We do not collect analytics or upload files automatically. Exported backups are passphrase-encrypted before being saved to the location you choose.
We have no servers. We do not see your files.
You are always in control.
