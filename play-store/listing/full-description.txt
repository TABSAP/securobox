SecuroBox is an on-device private media vault. Keep your videos, audio, photos and documents safe behind a PIN and biometric lock. Vault files stay on your device by default. You can optionally save a passphrase-encrypted backup to a location you choose, including a connected cloud drive; SecuroBox never uploads files automatically.

★ Why SecuroBox

• Offline vault storage by default — no account, analytics, or automatic uploads
• PIN + biometric (fingerprint / face) lock for the entire app
• Screenshot blocking — prevents screen recording and screen capture
• AES-256-CTR encryption for every file at rest
• PIN hashed with PBKDF2 + stored in OS Keystore
• No ads, no trackers, no analytics
• No account, no sign-up, no internet permission required for playback

★ What you can store

• Videos (MP4, MOV, MKV, AVI, WMV, FLV, M4V)
• Audio (MP3, WAV, AAC, FLAC, OGG, M4A, WMA)
• Photos (JPG, PNG, GIF, WEBP, BMP)
• Documents (PDF, DOC, DOCX, PPT, XLS, TXT)

★ Built-in players

• Video player with playback speed, fullscreen and gesture controls
• Audio player with background playback
• PDF reader with smooth scrolling and zoom
• Image gallery with pinch-to-zoom

★ Organized library

• Imported media is filed by type — Videos, Photos, Audio, Documents
• Extra built-in categories you can sort into: Educational, Personal, Work, Sports, Travel
• Create your own custom folders
• Quick search across your vault
• Recycle bin for accidentally deleted files
• Sort by newest imported, oldest imported, or name
• Lock selected categories with your PIN or biometrics

★ Import options

• Capture photos and videos straight into the vault with the in-app Secure Camera
• Pick existing files from your device storage
• Move-to-vault: imported photos and videos are removed from your gallery and kept safely in the vault — move them back out anytime
• Foreground album rules with a review queue and an option to keep Gallery originals
• Download from a direct URL into your vault
• Files are encrypted into a private app folder, isolated from the rest of your phone

★ Security model

• PIN with strength check (rejects 1234, 0000, sequential digits, repeats)
• Optional biometric unlock (fingerprint or face)
• Optional in-app Face Unlock
• Auto-lock when the app goes to background
• PIN hashed with PBKDF2 in the OS hardware-backed Keystore

★ Advanced protection

• Break-in detection — silently captures a photo of anyone who enters the wrong PIN, saved only inside your vault
• Disguise mode — hide the app behind a Calculator or other harmless icon (Android)
• Decoy PIN — open a separate decoy vault under duress while your real vault stays hidden
• Offline Integrity Lock — optionally seal the vault whenever the device is connected to any network
• Optional PIN recovery using a one-time code you set up
• Guest View lets you show only selected photos or videos and requires authentication to exit

★ Backup & recovery

• Create a portable backup protected by a passphrase
• Restore and re-encrypt your files on this or a new device
• Use the system file picker to save the backup to Files or a connected cloud drive
• Current backup limit: 128 MB of media

★ Privacy promise

We do not collect analytics or upload files automatically.
If you export a backup, it is passphrase-encrypted before it is saved to the location you choose.
We have no servers. We do not see your files.
You are in control — always.

SecuroBox is perfect for anyone who wants to keep personal videos, sensitive documents, or private memories safely tucked away on their phone, accessible only by them.
